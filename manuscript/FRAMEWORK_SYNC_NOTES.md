# Paper V2 framework synchronization

This note records the scientific boundary used to revise the first RBE paper.

## What Paper V2 now imports from the frozen RBE framework

- Resolvable Capability Outcome: `RCO=(C,E,P,D)`.
- Conservative Extension / Evidence Reuse: already resolution-adequate outcome evidence requires zero additional RBE assessment.
- Explicit distinction between one-step discriminating probes and a complete adaptive resolution-restoring policy/tree.
- Positive resolution requirement for Resolved Capability Attainment: low risk alone is insufficient; the resolved decision must support the positive certification decision.
- Operational terminal states: AR, AU, RN, NA, Deferred.
- Course metrics: PAR, RR, RAR, UAR, RNR/negative-resolution rate where applicable, DR and MRB.
- Programme compatibility-layer aggregation: PPO/RPO/UPO with caution that direct programme-level evidence remains necessary for integrative/high-stakes capabilities.
- 100-mark/approved performance assessment remains distinct from the Resolution Gate.

## What is intentionally left for a separate framework paper

The first scientific paper does not attempt to reproduce the entire institutional operating architecture. The following remain the subject of the separate complete-framework paper/specification:

- accreditation crosswalks and statutory implementation;
- detailed faculty workload and examiner systems;
- institution-wide governance and committees;
- data retention/security architecture;
- progression/graduation regulation;
- complete Capability Passport governance;
- detailed policy templates and accreditation evidence packs;
- cross-disciplinary deployment standards.

## Claim boundary

Paper V2 must not claim:

- that OBE is obsolete or incapable of rich assessment;
- that RBE invented authentic assessment, viva, adaptive testing, Bayesian updating, selective classification, experiment design, equivalence relations or Pareto analysis;
- that current OULAD evidence proves causal learning benefit, fairness, GenAI-specific validity or institutional superiority;
- that RBE has already been endorsed by Washington Accord/IEA, NBA, AICTE, UGC, ABET or other bodies.

The scientific claim is narrower: certification validity requires compatibility between the decision distinctions claimed by a credential and the resolution supplied by the assessment evidence; RBE operationalizes that object with selective, burden-aware evidence acquisition and explicit defer/abstention when evidence remains insufficient.
